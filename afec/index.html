01 About AFEC

From joint venture to a Lokma factory.

Amiantit Fiberglass Egypt was established in 1998 as a joint venture between Lokma Group and Saudi Arabia’s Amiantit. In 2021 Lokma acquired the Amiantit shares, bringing the factory fully into the group.

AFEC produces pipes by continuous filament winding under licence from VEM Technologies, an Italian specialist in complete GRP plant solutions. The range covers water, sewage, hydropower, municipal, industrial and marine applications, supported by specialists in the local and export markets.

AFEC technology

Within the VEM Technologies chain of licences, AFEC is a leading supplier of glass-reinforced plastic pipes. The process is continuously developed and offers several advantages.

  • 01

    Computerised production

    Consistent quality across every wound length.

  • 02

    Dual resin delivery

    Separate resins for liner and structure, including severe corrosive service.

  • 03

    Abrasion control

    Glass or polyester veil can be added to reduce wear.

  • 04

    High capacity

    Plant output supports large transmission programmes.

  • 05

    Cost effective

    Efficient winding for competitive project supply.

  • 06

    Flexible lengths

    Pipes from 3 to 12 metres to suit the route.

02 Products

GRP pipes, fittings and specials.

Pipes are produced in standard lengths of 6 to 12 metres, diameters from 100 mm to 4,000 mm, operating pressure up to 32 bar, and stiffness classes of 2,500, 5,000 and 10,000 N/mm². AFEC also manufactures fittings to standard and non-standard specifications, manholes, tanks for fuel and liquid chemicals, and pipe for jacking.

Applications

Transmission and distribution for drinking water, storm water, sanitation, industrial waste, chemicals and irrigation; cooling water lines for power and desalination plants; chemical process, down-hole tubing, ducting, geothermal, oil field, seawater intake and outfalls, slurry piping and water networks.

Advantages of fiberglass

  • 01

    No corrosion

    Products do not corrode and resist aggressive soils.

  • 02

    Stray-current immune

    Unaffected by stray current; no cathodic protection needed.

  • 03

    No lining or coating

    The pipe wall itself is the corrosion barrier.

  • 04

    Light weight

    Easier handling, transport and installation.

  • 05

    Smooth bore

    Low friction for efficient hydraulics.

  • 06

    Above or below ground

    Suitable for buried and exposed service with easy maintenance.
