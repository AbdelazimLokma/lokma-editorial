01 About CIPAC

A leading clay-pipe works in the Middle East.

CIPAC was established in 1981 to produce glazed vitrified clay pipes and fittings from 100 mm up to 1,000 mm with flexible joints for drains and sewers operating on gravity in municipal and industrial applications.

Pipes are manufactured according to Egyptian standard 56-2005 and European standard EN 295, which specify requirements for vitrified clay pipes and fittings with flexible rubber and polyurethane joints. Quality is monitored under ISO 9001:2008, ISO 14001:2004 and OHSAS 18001:2007.

CIPAC’s vision is to deliver superior pipe systems for wastewater, locally and globally, through commitment to standards, teamwork, responsible behaviour, learning and innovation.

02 Products

Vitrified clay pipes and fittings.

VCP is produced from clay, grog and water. Glaze is applied before firing; sintering at very high temperatures creates a material with exceptional chemical resistance, mechanical strength, impermeability and hardness. Annual capacity is around 35,000 tonnes. Diameters of 1,200 mm are planned.

Properties of vitrified clay

  • 01

    H₂S corrosion resistance

    Resists biologically induced hydrogen sulphide attack in sewers.

  • 02

    Chemical resistance

    Resistant to chemical attack across a wide pH range through the full wall thickness.

  • 03

    Mechanical strength

    Mechanical resistance has developed enormously over recent decades.

  • 04

    High-pressure cleaning

    Withstands jetting and unclogging better than many other materials.

  • 05

    Hydraulics

    Smooth bore (roughness 0.02–0.05 mm); usable on slopes up to 10 m/s.

  • 06

    Durability

    Service life calculated at 80 to 100 years even under hard conditions.

  • 07

    Abrasion resistance

    High abrasion resistance in both the glaze and the wall.

  • 08

    Temperature stability

    Low heat-expansion coefficient; compensating measures are usually unnecessary.

  • 09

    Environmentally sound

    Made from clay and recycled ceramic materials; long life reduces replacement.

  • 10

    Lower whole-life cost

    Long write-down period and lower maintenance, renovation and replacement costs.
