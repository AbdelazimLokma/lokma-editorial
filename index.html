Sheet 02

The lines under the city are the work.

Lokma began in 1955 as Arab Association, a contractor for major infrastructure. Egypt followed in 1975, and pipe manufacturing became the core: vitrified clay for gravity sewers, glass-reinforced plastic for transmission, and the engineering required to specify either one.

04 Our story

From one contractor to a pipe group.

  1. 1955

    Eng. Abd El Azim Lokma establishes the group in Saudi Arabia and the Arabian Gulf. Arab Association takes on major infrastructure.

  2. 1975

    The work begins in Egypt. Pipe manufacturing becomes the core business of the group.

  3. 1981

    CIPAC is established to produce glazed vitrified clay pipes and fittings.

  4. 1998

    AFEC is formed with Amiantit to manufacture GRP pipe by continuous filament winding.

  5. 2021

    Lokma acquires the remaining Amiantit holding. AFEC is fully within the group.
