01 Our companies

Three factories, one group.

Each company manufactures one pipe material under its own name and quality system. Together they cover pressure transmission, gravity drainage and large-diameter infrastructure.

  • 01

    AFEC

    Amiantit Fiberglass Egypt · GRP pipe · Est. 1998

    Glass-reinforced plastic pipes and fittings made by continuous filament winding, for drinking water, sewage, irrigation, desalination and industrial lines.

  • 02

    Ameron Egypt

03 Our story

From one contractor to a pipe group.

Eng. Abd El Azim Lokma founded the group in 1955 in Saudi Arabia and the Arabian Gulf, with Arab Association contracting major infrastructure. Work in Egypt began in 1975, and pipe manufacturing became the core of the group.

  1. 1955

    The group is founded. Arab Association takes on major infrastructure in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf.

  2. 1975

    Operations begin in Egypt. Pipe manufacturing becomes the core business.

  3. 1981

    CIPAC is established to produce vitrified clay pipes and fittings.

  4. 1996 – 1998

    Ameron Egypt and AFEC are formed with Saudi Amiantit, for concrete cylinder and GRP pipe.

  5. 2021

    Lokma acquires the Amiantit shares. All three factories are fully within the group.
