01 About Ameron Egypt

Bar-wrapped concrete cylinder pipe since 1996.

Ameron Egypt was established as a joint venture between Lokma Group and the Saudi Arabian Amiantit Company in 1996. The company manufactures concrete cylinder pipes according to the international quality standard AWWA C303.

In 2021 Lokma Group acquired the Saudi Amiantit shares and took full ownership. Compliance is monitored through an internal quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2008, ISO 14001:2004 and OHSAS 18001:2007.

02 Products

Concrete cylinder pipe and factory fittings.

CCP is used principally for the transmission and distribution of water in municipal, industrial and irrigation systems. It is also used in sewage force mains and seawater cooling systems. Fittings are manufactured in the plant to eliminate cutting and fit-up in the field.
